Meadow Market Littles: engagement through meaningful play (Build 46)

Design objective: children want to return because they enjoy making, helping,
discovering and mastering something. App engagement is not evidence of learning.
These changes require child/family usability work before claiming learning gains.

Evidence consulted 8 November 2026:
NAEYC, Principles of Child Development and Learning.
https://www.naeyc.org/resources/position-statements/dap/principles
Relevant principles: choice, wonder, delight, active meaningful engagement,
supported challenge and learning through exploration and social play.

Skene et al., Child Development (2022), systematic review/meta-analysis,
DOI 10.1111/cdev.13730. 39 studies reviewed; 17 in meta-analysis.
https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/90660/1/Child_Development_2022_Skene_Can_guidance_during_play_enhance_children_s_learning_and_development_in_educational.pdf
Guided play showed advantages for selected early maths, shape knowledge,
task switching and spatial vocabulary comparisons. Other outcomes did not show
differences. This does not validate this app or support a blanket educational claim.

Harvard Center on the Developing Child, Brain-Building Through Play (2022),
and activities for 4-7-year-olds.
https://developingchild.harvard.edu/resources/handouts-tools/brainbuildingthroughplay/
https://developingchild.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/5-Games-Activities-for-Children-4-7-years.pdf
Use age/ability appropriate challenges, physical play, memory, turn taking,
and opportunities for adults to guide then allow growing independence.

Applied design decisions:
- Optional picture-led farm jobs offer a helping, thinking and creating choice.
  All catalogue games stay available. Recent choices vary the invitations.
- Play has a meaningful visible outcome: a fitted window, stirred recipe,
  performing animal tune, movable saved farm/story, uncovered farm friend.
- Forgiving targets, deliberate actions, duplicate-tap locks and optional hints.
- A short shared celebration adds animated golden stars, clear farmer praise
  already approved in the game, and a gently glowing next/replay control.
- A clear All done/Back control remains available. No expiry, loss of a streak,
  randomized prize schedule or automatic relaunch is introduced.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove decorative movement.
- Saved creations and compositions provide reasons to revisit and experiment.

Validation: automated regression checks plus catalogue browser play testing.
Separate browser/device QA records report the actual extent of coverage.
