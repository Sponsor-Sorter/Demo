# Meadow Markets build 185 — phone story layout

The physical Samsung check of build184 found that the two-row story header and large introduction left too little room for goals in landscape. This update places the title, chapter selector, Daily jobs and close control on one row on short landscape screens, reduces the introduction height and keeps the goal area independently scrollable. Portrait layout and all 35 existing goals are retained.

Build184's reward/recovery changes and checks remain applicable. No further backend or authentication change is made. Purchases and ads remain free test systems. See meadow-market-build184.md for the security scope and the outstanding owner-entered real password-reset test.
