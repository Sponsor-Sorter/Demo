# Littles70 — growing challenges and helpful hints

Spot the Difference grows through nine difficulty stages. New learners begin with four large details and one missing item; later levels add more details and substitute different objects. The largest challenge has eight details and five changes. The level chooser offers an easier or harder challenge without shrinking touch targets or introducing a timer.

Two incorrect attempts on the current step reveal a glowing hint button. Incorrect guesses, wrong destinations and completed drags that miss a destination count; cancelled or incomplete gestures do not. A hint highlights one valid answer or the current restoration destination, never completing it or granting a reward. Correct steps clear the miss count and previous highlight. The same guidance is recorded as a standing rule for future puzzle upgrades.

Existing records, game ID, earned progress and completion/allowance rules stay in use. Accepted69 behaviour remains active for public players until private70 phone acceptance passes. Four new, short approved Rhys/Noah recordings describe changed pictures, placement, hints and completion; the prior next-scene recording remains appropriate.

Validation covers900 complete seeded rounds across all nine stages, hint gating and resets, free wrong moves, unique unambiguous substitutions, invalid inputs and legacy69. Browser geometry, full rounds, native acceptance and exact public byte checks are recorded in the private release evidence and final PR metadata.
