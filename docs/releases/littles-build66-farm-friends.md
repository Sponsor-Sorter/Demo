Six older activities now use direct gestures, visible movement and a short practical task after remembering or listening. Existing game IDs, save records, approved preview illustrations, completed-round accounting and rewards are retained.

- Farm Friend Pairs: match three or four animal pairs, then bring each friend to its picture home. Square card fronts and backs stay mounted; no image reload on each turn.
- Who’s Missing?: look at three friends, choose Ready, then drag the missing animal back. Look again preserves the same lesson. Three gentle rounds with a visible reunion between them.
- Copy the Pattern: watch two or three foods light up on train wagons, then load them in order. Each spoken food finishes before the next; the loaded train drives away.
- Who Made That Sound?: hear an animal, pull its picture door open, then stroke the friend as it steps out. Three animals; picture clues support younger children and muted play. A delayed sound belongs only to the round that scheduled it.
- Moving Harvest: drag six requested foods from a slowly moving farm bench into the basket, then carry the basket to the cart. Held food follows the finger while other foods keep moving; misses are free.
