# Littles 80 — growing learning adventures

The ten existing learning activities retain their IDs, illustrated full-screen farm scenes and real water, train, picnic, trolley, shape, harvest, bridge, hiding, tractor and dancing animations. Four bounded challenge milestones (1, 4, 9, 16) replace the two learner options for the upgraded experience. Families can return to an easier challenge. Completed rounds remember the next learning challenge without changing existing wins, records, cloud identity or allowance rules.

- Water the Garden increases the loose gutters and length of the connected path.
- Sound Train adds distractor letters while retaining familiar three-letter words and their approved phonics.
- Picnic Fair Shares increases friends and portions, keeping individual food pieces reachable. Full plates reject another piece with useful feedback.
- Rolling Rescue increases missing ramps and introduces an alternating uphill/downhill route.
- Patchwork Animals increases simultaneous shape choices and required rotations.
- Orchard Add and Take Away grows the small quantities and offers closer number choices, within the existing approved zero-to-six recordings.
- Rhyming River adds more picture alternatives.
- Where’s Pip grows from one clue and two hiding places to three successive spoken clues in each adventure.
- Tractor Coding grows the field and obstacles.
- Freeze Dance increases deliberate dancing and stillness gently, with generous settling time and no failed round.

Two genuine incorrect attempts on the current step offer a glowing hint button. Only requesting it highlights one useful piece, destination, gutter orientation or program edit. The child performs the move; a hint neither completes the puzzle nor grants a reward. Correct steps reset help. Invalid, duplicate, cancelled and incomplete input remains free. Freeze guidance counts completed moving gestures, with small wiggles tolerated.

The intro uses the approved light text and shadow directly over the artwork, without a coloured instruction panel. Hint controls remain inside the scene, with stable touch areas and a glow rather than moving hit boxes. Existing independent Jade/Hope, Rhys/Noah and Jay/Mike recordings match the expanded tasks; their approved hint recordings are reused. No new speech generation is needed.

The existing quality gate keeps public mechanics 77 until installed-app acceptance of the private 80 candidate. Initial local validation includes the original 3,000 learning tasks, 4,800 progressive tasks, 20 complete browser rounds and 200 responsive challenge cases. A failed-flow animation result and overlapping picnic/hiding touch areas were corrected during QA. Final native acceptance, exact publication bytes and public activation are recorded in the release evidence and PR after observation. This content update does not submit an Android binary or claim Google Play approval.
