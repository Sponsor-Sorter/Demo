# Build 42 — Find friends farm adventures

Find the Lambs, Find the Goats and Find the Cows now share a complete hands-on adventure while retaining distinct animal artwork, movement and calls.

The child moves illustrated leaves, lifts a hay bale or basket lid, or opens a little gate. Animals peek and blink behind their covers. Opening an empty hiding place releases a butterfly; opening an occupied one reveals an animal to tap. Lambs bound, goats hop and cows walk into the waiting group. Farmer Rhys offers a recorded sound clue with a rustling hiding place and the matching animal call.

After everyone gathers, the child opens the paddock gate and follows the curved footprint path with a finger. Animals follow the tracing progress towards their little barn. Food is then scattered by dragging a scoop through the trough. The animals visibly munch, Rhys congratulates the child, and the replay screen appears after his recording finishes. Keyboard and select-then-tap alternatives are included. There is no countdown or failure state.

Starter rounds contain three animals. Later rounds progress to four, five and six, with more concealed peeking poses. Six large hiding places are shuffled, positions vary, coat variants change, and replay rotates to another of the three backgrounds. Rhys’s new recordings use the approved **Noah - YA Audiobooks Narrator** voice and Eleven Multilingual v2. Device speech is removed from these three games.

Validation: full browser playthroughs of lambs, goats and cows; real cover drags, animal collection, keyboard route, curved finger route in multiple strokes, movement-based feeding, hint and butterfly, pause mid-walk and replay scene rotation. Checked 320×568 and 390×844 portrait phones, 844×390 landscape phone, and 820×1180 tablet. No app console errors. All eight MP3 files fully decoded. Regression tests cover 120 varied rounds, guaranteed scene rotation, correct three-to-six animal counts, deliberate tracing, sparse pointer segments, no progress from holding a scoop still or moving outside the trough, and forgiving near-full feeding. Existing Littles checks also pass.

## Saved artwork and prompts

Generated using the built-in image generation tool, then copied into the game as WebP assets:

- `meadow-market-littles/assets/generated/minigames/find-meadow.webp`
- `meadow-market-littles/assets/generated/minigames/find-orchard.webp`
- `meadow-market-littles/assets/generated/minigames/find-barnyard.webp`

The exact three prompts are saved in `meadow-market-littles/assets/generated/minigames/find-art-generation.json`. All request a simple full-bleed farm scene with a clear grassy play area, soft storybook shapes and no text, animals or controls baked into the background. The meadow uses distant barn and trees, the orchard uses apple trees and fence, and the barnyard uses the red barn and fence. Interactive foreground objects and articulated animals are native SVG, drawn by `find-adventure.js`.

The three self-contained preview cards are generated by `tools/build-littles-find-cards.cjs`. The audio script, split boundaries, exact text, voice provenance and sizes are stored in `assets/audio/find-generation.json`; the repeatable importer is `tools/import-littles-find-audio.cjs`.
