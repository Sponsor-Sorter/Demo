Meadow Market Littles

Privacy policy

Meadow Market Littles is published by Fairplay Gaming for young children and their families. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Play and creative work

Game progress is saved on your device. Paintings stay in device storage unless you choose to export them. The name used in Write My Name is held only for that activity; it is not saved or sent to our server.

Local two-player games connect directly on your Wi-Fi. Optional camera access scans pairing codes on the device. Camera frames are not uploaded. You can paste a pairing code instead. The app does not request precise location, contacts or microphone access.

Grown-up accounts and cloud saves

Our Supabase service stores the account identifier and game progress needed for cloud saving. A grown-up can optionally link and verify an email for recovery. Account access, moderation and technical security records help us protect the service. We do not ask children to provide an email, birth date or real name to play. Account and purchase settings are behind a grown-up check.

Advertising in the Android app

Where advertising is enabled, the Android app uses Google AdMob for one labelled advertisement on the game-selection screen. We configure child-directed treatment, a G content limit and no personalised ads or remarketing, and remove the Android advertising-ID permission. We do not send a child’s name, drawings or parent login to the ad request.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can process IP-derived general location, ad interactions, diagnostics and permitted device or app identifiers for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Disabling the advertising ID does not mean no technical data is processed. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s SDK disclosure. Advertising privacy choices, where required, are available in grown-up settings.

Subscriptions

Google Play processes the payment method; we do not receive card details. Our server checks the purchase token with Google and stores the account binding, a token hash, subscription state and expiry. Signed entitlement proofs allow the app to recognise Plus. Complimentary access, when granted by the owner, is recorded separately from paid purchases. We do not sell children’s personal information.

Retention and deletion

Cloud information is retained to provide the account and saved progress until deletion is requested. Necessary transaction, security or anti-fraud records may be retained where required, separately from playable child progress. Google and our hosting providers have their own service and backup retention practices.

Request deletion of the Littles account and associated data. A grown-up can use the same link in settings. Removing the app does not cancel a Google subscription or necessarily delete a cloud save. Shared Fairplay login or other games are not erased by a Littles-only request; tell us if you also want the shared account deleted.

Contact

For privacy, child-data access/correction or deletion requests, email meadowmarkets@sponsorsorter.com. Please identify Meadow Market Littles and use the grown-up account email; do not send a child’s name or picture.
