# Littles play quality

These are the user's standing requirements for this children's game, for ages roughly3–6.

- Use full-screen illustrated farm scenes, with all controls inside the scene. Keep large, separate touch targets of at least44CSSpixels on compact phones. More difficult levels must not make controls too small or crowded.
- Completion-based matching, memory and observation puzzles need genuine difficulty progression: more choices, longer patterns, more details or closer alternatives, within preschool-friendly limits. Let players return to an easier challenge. Creative free play does not need incorrect-answer rules.
- After two genuine incorrect attempts on the current step, offer a clearly glowing hint button. Tapping it should highlight the correct choice, matching card or destination. It must not automatically complete the answer, award progress, charge a try or reveal unrelated answers. Reset the miss counter and hint after a correct step. A cancelled gesture is not a wrong guess.

Coordinate the shared phone and publishing windows; preserve other apps, device settings, accounts and saved data.
