Meadow Market Littles

Meadow Littles Plus

Published by Fairplay Gaming. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What Plus includes

Plus removes in-app advertising and provides unlimited completed rounds. Free play includes five completed rounds of each game per day. Weekly farm-fair trophies can award extra free rounds for each game; these bonus rounds expire at the end of that UTC week. Failed attempts do not use a round. The daily allowance follows the device’s local calendar day. Creative pictures and existing progress remain available without a subscription.

Monthly payment

The Australian reference price is A$4.99 per month. Before subscribing, the app displays the price returned by Google Play. Google’s checkout shows the actual amount and any local tax treatment. The monthly subscription renews automatically until cancelled. There is no advertised free trial or introductory offer.

Purchases are for grown-ups

Open grown-up settings and use a verified, recoverable parent account before buying. Google Play handles checkout. Plus unlocks after our server verifies the purchase; a pending or cancelled payment does not unlock it. A local game-save flag cannot establish a paid entitlement.

Restore, cancel and refunds

Use Restore purchases with the same Google Play account and Littles parent account on a compatible device. Use Manage Google Play subscription in grown-up settings, or Google Play subscriptions, to cancel. Cancellation stops future renewals; verified access continues through the paid period. Expiry, payment hold, refund or revocation can end access as determined by Google’s current subscription state.

Uninstalling Littles or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel the Google subscription. Google’s purchase and refund processes apply. For help resolving a verified purchase, contact meadowmarkets@sponsorsorter.com. Do not send card details.

Complimentary access

Owner-granted access is separate from a paid subscription. Revoking a complimentary grant does not cancel an independently active Google subscription, and a refunded purchase does not remove an independently active complimentary grant.
