Delete your Littles account and data

Meadow Market Littles · Fairplay Gaming · Android package com.meadowmarket.littles

Request account deletion

A grown-up can request deletion without installing or reopening the game. Send a request from the parent account email using the button below. Identify Meadow Market Littles; do not include a child’s name or drawings. We may verify account ownership before deleting data.

Request deletion by email

You can also email meadowmarkets@sponsorsorter.com directly with subject “Delete Meadow Market Littles account”.

What the request covers

The Littles profile, cloud progress and account-associated game data are included. Necessary billing, security or anti-fraud records may need limited retention. Google retains its own payment records. Drawings saved only on a device are not in our cloud: remove them from the device separately, or clear the app’s storage after exporting any pictures you want to keep.

A Littles-only request does not delete other Fairplay games or their shared login. If you want the entire shared Fairplay account deleted, state that separately so support can explain the effect on other games.

Check your subscription

Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not cancel Google Play billing. You can manage or cancel your Google Play subscription here. Contact support if you need help with a paid account or cannot access the parent email.
