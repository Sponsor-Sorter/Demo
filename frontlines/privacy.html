Privacy and your commander account

Frontlines: World in Conflict is operated by Fairplay Gaming. This page explains the information used to run the game, online play and optional cosmetic purchases. Contact frontlines@sponsorsorter.com for privacy questions.

Playing and signing in

The game can store your settings, commander appearance, downloaded content and solo progress on your device. Online services use an account identifier, display name, appearance, friends, match actions, results, campaign progress and rankings. Other players can see your public commander identity and game activity. Your email address is used for registered-account sign-in and recovery; it is not a public commander field.

Supabase provides account authentication and game database services. Passwords are handled by the authentication service. The app can retain a protected sign-in credential so you remain signed in between sessions and updates; your password is not saved by the game. Signing out removes the remembered sign-in on that device.

Purchases, gems and VIP

Optional purchases provide cosmetic items and permanent VIP. Google Play handles payment details in the Play edition. The game verifies purchase receipts and keeps a server-controlled balance and ownership record. We retain purchase identifiers, receipt hashes and grant/refund history to deliver purchases, restore ownership and prevent duplicate awards or fraud. Payment card details are not provided to the game.

Advertising

Mobile advertising is controlled by the game's release configuration. When enabled, Google AdMob and its consent tools provide optional rewarded ads and ads between completed matches. No ad is intended to interrupt a live battle or training lesson. Permanent VIP removes automatic ads. Rewarded ads remain optional and rewards require server verification.

Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic information according to the choices and restrictions available on your device. Advertising privacy choices are available from the Commander Collection screen in supported mobile editions. See Google's privacy policy.

Support, security and service operation

Support messages contain the information you choose to send. Connection and service records can include IP addresses, request timing and errors used to operate and protect the service. Access to account, purchase and moderation tools is restricted to authorised staff. Incoming support email does not itself authorise a purchase, grant, ban or account change. Never send passwords, payment card details or purchase tokens to support.

Your choices and deletion

You can change your public commander name and appearance, sign out, adjust audio/graphics settings and request deletion of your Frontlines account data. Use Commander Account → Privacy & Account Deletion in the game, or the account deletion website. Support verifies ownership before processing and confirms completion. You can cancel a pending request.

Deletion covers the Frontlines profile, custom appearance, progress, friend relationships and identifiable game history associated with the request. Information belonging to another game or app is handled separately. Purchase, fraud-prevention and other records that must be retained for legal obligations may be kept separately with restricted access. Support can explain any retention that applies to your request.

Retention and changes

Account data is used while you maintain an account or while needed to provide the service, resolve support issues and meet applicable obligations. This policy will be updated when the game's services or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the game or its website.
