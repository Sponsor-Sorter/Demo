Our farm community

Fairplay Gaming · Rules version 1 · 30 October 2026

Meadow Markets is for players aged 13 and over. Keep this a welcoming place to grow and play.

  • Use friendly farm names. No hate, sexual content, threats, harassment or impersonation.
  • Do not publish passwords, contact details or other personal information.
  • No scams, exploit abuse, cheating or attempts to access another player’s account.
  • Respect other players. A friend visit is permission for the game’s limited helping actions only.

Report or block

Use Report / block on a farm’s Friends card, during a visit or in your visitor log. A report includes the reported farm, your game account, the reason and any optional details. Do not include sensitive information. Reports go to Fairplay Gaming for review; reporting does not automatically punish a player.

Blocking removes your farm connection and pending help, and prevents farm viewing and new invitations between those accounts. Manage your blocks in Settings → Community & blocked farms. Unblocking does not restore friendship.

Review and enforcement

We review reports and may remove an inappropriate name or restrict social access when rules are broken. For urgent concerns, an appeal or a report you cannot send in the game, contact meadowmarkets@sponsorsorter.com. Reports are normally kept for no more than 180 days. See our privacy policy.
