The initial eight-object concept atlas is preserved at `source/farm-art-sheet.png`; source files are not copied into the runtime bundle. Dedicated crops, coop, hen, tree, and stall sprites are individual transparent images used by the game. Emoji remain as temporary fallback art for content that still needs dedicated final illustrations (other crops, animal breeds, vendors, and small decorations); those are tracked as remaining art work rather than represented as finished generated assets.

Assets were generated for this project on 2026-10-18 and then sized for in-game display. Before release, review them on target devices for edge quality, readability, and asset-specific pivots.
