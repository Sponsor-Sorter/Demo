Meadow Markets privacy policy

Fairplay Gaming · Updated 30 October 2026

Meadow Markets is a farming game for players aged 13 and over. Meadow Market Littles is a separate game with separate privacy information. This policy describes Meadow Markets on the web and Android.

Your game information

We store your account identifier, chosen username and farm name, cloud farm save, progress, inventory, friend connections, visits and help history, game scores and reward records. Accepted friends can see your shared farm. The farm directory shows farm names and invite codes so players can find each other. Do not use personal information in public names.

Account authentication and game data are hosted by Supabase. Passwords are handled by the authentication service; we do not show or retain plaintext passwords. Recovery keys are shown once and stored only as a verification hash. Keep your key private. Authentication abuse controls retain salted IP-derived attempt counters for a short period, normally 15 minutes.

Purchases and advertising

Where enabled, Android purchases use Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we receive purchase tokens, product identifiers, purchase state and transaction references to verify delivery, restore entitlements and handle refunds. We do not receive your full card details. Virtual items have no cash value.

Where enabled and selected by you, advertisements use Google AdMob. Google may process device and network information for ad delivery, fraud prevention and measurement. Our requests are configured for restricted, nonpersonalised advertising. Applicable privacy choices are presented before requesting ads. Premium removes optional popup advertisements. Rewarded ads are voluntary and rewards are delivered only after verification. Advertisement availability varies by device and region.

Purchases and live ads are not available until the production store configuration is enabled. Approved playtest accounts may receive free test supplies; test entitlements are not payments.

Devices and reliability

Saved sessions, farm caches, preferences and safety backups are stored on your device. The game downloads artwork, audio and content from sponsorsorter.com and uses standard network hosting logs. Optional error reporting sends only the game build, module name, line number and a generic error category associated with your game account, not screenshots, passwords, recovery keys or typed content.

How we use and share data

We use game data to operate saves, account recovery, friends, leaderboards, rewards and support; prevent fraud; and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information. Our infrastructure and payment/advertising providers process the information needed to deliver their services. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the regions used by those providers.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from Settings → Privacy & account deletion, or on our account deletion page. A seven-day cancellation window protects against mistakes. After that, Meadow Markets farm, username, recovery records, friend links and game activity are removed. Limited transaction and security records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations or fraud prevention. Private backup copies expire within 30 days. If an underlying technical sign-in identity also serves another app, deletion of Meadow Markets does not delete that app or its account.

A hashed technical identifier is retained to prevent an old session from restoring deleted game data. You can clear local game data through your browser or Android settings. Clearing device data alone does not delete a cloud account. You can export a farm backup from game Settings. Contact us to request access to or correction of your information or to raise a privacy concern.
