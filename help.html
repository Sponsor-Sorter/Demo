Getting started

Which account should I choose?

Choose sponsor if you are a brand or person offering content partnerships. Choose sponsee if you are a creator producing the sponsored content. Creators can select Free or Pro; sponsors use Pro. Create your account or compare plans.

Can I try a dashboard first?

Yes. Explore the sponsor preview or creator preview. These use fictional data and do not create real sponsorships.

What should I include in my profile?

Use a recognisable image, a clear introduction, your location and the platforms where you publish. Creators should explain their content and audience; brands should explain their business and the partnerships they want.

Offers & campaigns

How do I find a partner?

Use the Finder to search profiles, browse public offers or discover groups. Check a potential partner’s content and profile before discussing a campaign. Creating a profile does not guarantee a sponsorship.

What should a sponsorship offer include?

Set out the content deliverables, publishing platforms, amount, deadline and payment schedule. Discuss usage rights, approvals and any exclusivity before accepting. Use the offer’s comments to keep the discussion with the campaign.

How do I follow campaign progress?

Your dashboard groups offers by status. Open an offer to review its details, comments and content updates. The workflow moves from the offer being sent and accepted through content creation, publication and completion.

What if something changes or there is a disagreement?

Discuss the change with your partner through the offer’s comments. Keep a record of the agreed requirements and any updates. Use the available reporting tools for concerning content or contact support. Read the Terms of Service for the applicable rules.

Accounts & platforms

I cannot log in. What can I do?

Check that you are using the email address you registered with. Use Forgot your password? on the login page to request a reset link. Never send your password or verification codes to another user.

How do social account connections work?

Available platforms provide different connection methods and profile information. Use the connection controls in your account and review the permissions requested by the platform. Some platforms use a manually entered handle. A displayed handle alone is not proof of account ownership.

My platform connection is not working.

Confirm that you are signed into the intended social account and that you meet that platform’s account requirements. Retry from your account’s connection controls. If it still fails, contact support with the platform name and error message. Do not include passwords, access tokens or verification codes.

How do I ask about my personal data?

Read our privacy policy and contact us with your request. Do not post private account information in public profiles, comments or the community forum.

Plans & payments

How much does Sponsor Sorter cost?

The creator Free plan costs US$0/month. Pro costs US$10/month for creator or sponsor accounts. Sponsorship offers carry a 10% platform fee paid up front by the sponsor. Review the selected plan and final charges at checkout. View pricing.

Where do I complete payment?

Paid signup opens Stripe’s hosted checkout. Review the subscription details and amount there before paying. If checkout fails or you cannot access your account afterwards, contact support with the account email and payment reference. Do not share card details.

Does the platform guarantee earnings or a successful campaign?

No. Partners decide which offers to make and accept. Review the requirements, amount and timing before committing to a campaign, and read the terms for payment and account conditions.
