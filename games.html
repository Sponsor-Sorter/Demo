Bargain Barge

Android testing release

Version 0.4.4 · Build 19

Browse Australian and New Zealand retailer offers and community deals, compare listed prices and open retailer pages in this Android testing app.

Get the Android app

Android 8.0 or newer. Internet connection for live feeds. Location permission is optional; you can choose a location manually.

How to get started
  1. Download the Android APK on your phone and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, approve installation from your downloading app, then install and open Bargain Barge.
  3. Select Australia or New Zealand and choose a location or enter a suburb.
  4. Search for an item, filter results and open an offer to refresh its source details. Confirm the final price, delivery and availability with the retailer.

Android app only; there is no browser version. Listed prices are not a guarantee of branch stock or final checkout totals. App updates require your Android installation confirmation.

Frontlines: World in Conflict

Web and Android playtest preview

Version 1.0.0-preview.33 · Build 33

Build 33 adds the approved Court Diplomat: tailored coat, silk lapels, jacquard waistcoat, modelled buttons and embroidered cuffs, with a matching clothing tile. Build 32 adds the approved HD Corsair coat, independently removable neckerchief and matching clothing-selector thumbnail. Build 31 adds the HD Expedition Captain, independent cravat and bandolier, new hair, hats, moustache, brows, lashes and age controls. Current HD cosmetics are included in the base game. Build 30 adds the approved HD Royal Guard coat, an independently removable handkerchief and full gold sash with blue edging and fitted buckle, Royal Curls and two starting presets. It retains all 18 garments, collection navigation, the approved HD Field Marshal and Grand Admiral with fixed lower cross. The 3D commander creator retains 64 grouped face controls and two-sided hair. Saved portraits use a canonical front view independent of editor rotation. Solo play supports autosave/resume, saves completed actions immediately and retains thermal protection.

Play browser previewDownload Android APK (manual install)

WebGL 2 and WebAssembly browser, or Android 7.0+ (64-bit ARM). Internet is needed for first load, online play and updates. Phone play is best in landscape.

How to play
  1. Android testing: download the versioned APK from GitHub Releases, open it on your Android device and allow installation if prompted. Install future testing builds manually from this page until Google Play distribution is ready.
  2. Build 33: open Your Commander, choose Wardrobe and Court Diplomat. The matching clothing tile is included. Neckwear and medals remain independently selectable; choose None to remove optional accessories.
  3. Build 32: open Your Commander, choose Wardrobe and Corsair. Its matching clothing tile is included. Select Corsair Neckerchief under Neckwear, or None, independently from the coat. Current HD cosmetics are bundled in the base game.
  4. Open Your Commander, choose Wardrobe, Uniforms & coats and Royal Guard. Choose Guard Handkerchief under Neckwear and Guard Gold Sash under Sash independently, or select None. Royal Curls is under Hair; Sentinel and Royal Envoy are under Presets. Save Commander applies your portrait.
  5. Open Your Commander, choose Wardrobe and Grand Admiral. The lower cross and black fastening belong to the coat. Select or remove shoulders, sash, cords and naval medals separately; current HD accessories are bundled in the base game; then save your commander.
  6. Open Your Commander, choose Wardrobe, then select a clothing collection. Browse all 18 outfits, rotate the preview, and save your commander.
  7. In Game Modes & Rules, choose Main World or one of seven prehistoric maps. Download an optional map once (about 7.7–10.6 MB); after verification, choose Use Map. Map Credits lists its sources. Home reframes the battlefield around the controls.
  8. Open Play browser preview, choose your game setup, then launch. Set human commanders to zero to watch AI play.
  9. Choose victory and reinforcement rules with the in-game touch pickers before launching. Adjust sound and dice finishes in Settings.
  10. Follow turn prompts to reinforce, attack and move troops. Choose solo AI, local hot-seat or online play.
  11. After occupation, an army that can attack stays selected. A compulsory fifth-card or inherited-hand trade returns the same commander to troop placement; place the trade troops, then continue the turn.
  12. Desktop: wheel to zoom, middle drag to pan, right drag to orbit. On phone, use landscape, drag to pan and pinch to zoom.
  13. Android: download the preview APK, open the downloaded file and confirm installation if Android asks. Android Back pauses or resumes the campaign.
  14. Local campaigns autosave on this device. Use Continue Campaign to continue; clearing game or browser data can remove the save.
  15. Use Field cards to inspect your hand and trade. Choose dice finishes in Settings; use the in-game speed controls for solo or AI spectator play.
  16. Choose Your Commander from the main menu, or Customise in Commander Account. Edit the 3D appearance, then Save Commander to use its portrait; Cancel offers a choice to discard unsaved changes.
  17. Open the card-fan button to inspect your earned hand. Local AI pauses while cards are open; Close resumes play. Shared online tables continue normally.
  18. In the creator, drag to rotate and tilt, use two fingers to pan/pinch, or use the desktop wheel to zoom and right/middle drag to pan. Reset View restores the front composition; Save Commander always captures a front portrait.
  19. Scroll account, creator, Settings and option lists with a vertical swipe. Swipe vertically over a slider to scroll; drag horizontally to change its value. Tap a field to enter text.
  20. Use the Face group menu for cheeks, jaw/chin, nose and other facial controls. Gender and Voice are in Presets; voice is independent of appearance. Preview Voice plays the selected set; adjust Commander volume in Settings.
  21. Open Pose to choose an editor animation, or Still to stop it; Replay restarts the action. Saved portraits keep their front composition. Custom commanders can use the player-card panel for limited public or private emotes.
