Early access

Browser build 5

Explore farm adventures, animal care, puzzles and art with Farmer Jade, Farmer Rhys and Farmer Jay. Play four two-player games on one device or pair directly across home Wi-Fi.

Play in browserDownload Android APK

Modern browser on phone, tablet or computer, or the Android testing package. Works in portrait or landscape. Internet access is needed for cloud saves and updates.

How to play and install
  1. Open Play in browser and choose an activity.

Bargain Barge

Android testing release

Version 0.4.4 · Build 19

Browse Australian and New Zealand retailer offers and community deals, compare listed prices and open retailer pages in this Android testing app.

Get the Android app

Android 8.0 or newer. Internet connection for live feeds. Location permission is optional; you can choose a location manually.

How to get started
  1. Download the Android APK on your phone and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, approve installation from your downloading app, then install and open Bargain Barge.
  3. Select Australia or New Zealand and choose a location or enter a suburb.
  4. Search for an item, filter results and open an offer to refresh its source details. Confirm the final price, delivery and availability with the retailer.

Android app only; there is no browser version. Listed prices are not a guarantee of branch stock or final checkout totals. App updates require your Android installation confirmation.

Frontlines: World in Conflict

Web and Android playtest preview

Version 1.0.0-preview.8 · Build 8

Command six coalitions across a raised 3D world board. Reinforce, roll and capture territories in solo AI, local hot-seat or online campaigns, with Capitals rules and private alliances.

Play browser previewDownload Android preview APK

WebGL 2 and WebAssembly browser, or Android 7.0+ (64-bit ARM). Internet is needed for first load, online play and updates. Phone play is best in landscape.

How to play
  1. Open Play browser preview, choose your game setup and launch the campaign.
  2. Follow turn prompts to reinforce, attack and move troops. Choose solo AI, local hot-seat or online play.
  3. Desktop: wheel to zoom, middle drag to pan, right drag to orbit. On phone, use landscape, drag to pan and pinch to zoom.
  4. Android: download the preview APK, open the downloaded file and confirm installation if Android asks. Android Back pauses or resumes the campaign.
  5. Campaigns are not saved persistently. Finish your campaign before closing or installing an update.

Playtest preview. Android build 8 was checked on a Samsung SM-S908E; other devices and sustained performance remain under review. No persistent campaign resume or remembered login. Android installation requires your confirmation; the in-app update flow is still being tested. Phone-to-phone optical QR and human email/friend flows remain pending. No paid cosmetic shop.
