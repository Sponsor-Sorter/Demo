    Get the Sponsor Sorter Android app for your phone or tablet. Find partners, manage offers and send content for approval with your existing account.

    Download for Android

    Android 7.0 or newer · Phones and tablets · Internet connection required

    Download Android app

    Version 1.0.0 · Build 8 · Signed Android APK · Early access

    Install in three steps

    1. Tap Download Android app on your Android phone or tablet.
    2. Open the downloaded APK. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app, then confirm Install. You can turn that browser permission off afterwards.
    3. Open Sponsor Sorter and sign in with your existing account.

    Keep Google Play Protect enabled. If your phone blocks installation, use the website and contact us rather than disabling its protection.

    Why does Android show an installation warning?

    This early-access app is downloaded directly rather than through Google Play. Android requires permission for your browser to install downloaded apps and may offer a Play Protect scan. Signing identifies the app’s publisher; it does not remove these checks or guarantee that an app is free of bugs.

    Download only using the button on this page. Keep Play Protect enabled and allow its scan. If installation remains blocked, use the website and tell us the warning shown. Do not disable your phone’s security protection.

    Prefer the website or use an iPhone?

    The Android download is for Android only. On iPhone or iPad, use the website in Safari; Share → Add to Home Screen adds the web version.

    Browser installation adds the web version, not the Android app.

    Updates, handled

    The Android app checks for compatible updates when opened and when you return to it. Updates download automatically while online and apply on the next full launch. Your upload, payment or unfinished message will not be interrupted.

    For a new Android package, return here and install the latest signed APK over the existing app. Android asks you to confirm; your account stays in place.

    Use your existing account

    Sign in on your phone, tablet and website. Multiple signed-in sessions are currently supported. Signing out in the updated Android app affects that device only.

    In the app’s Settings, App updates shows the running release and lets you check for updates.
