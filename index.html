A few things to know

What is a sponsor or a sponsee?

A sponsor is the brand or person offering a paid content partnership. A sponsee is the creator receiving the offer and producing the agreed content. You’ll see these names in the dashboards.

Do I need a large following to join?

You can create a profile without a minimum audience size. Each sponsor decides whether your content, audience and proposed partnership suit their campaign. Joining does not guarantee sponsorship or earnings.

Can I look around before signing up?

Yes. Open a creator preview or sponsor preview to explore a dashboard with demonstration data. You’ll need your own account to manage real sponsorships.

Who decides the content and sponsorship amount?

The sponsor sets out the proposed amount, deliverables, platforms and deadline in an offer. The creator should review all the details before accepting. Keep requirements clear and use the offer’s comments to discuss questions.

Is Sponsor Sorter a mobile app?

Sponsor Sorter is a website you can use in your browser on desktop, tablet or phone. A dedicated mobile app is planned; it is not required to use the website.

Where can I get help or raise a concern?

Use the help centre for guidance or contact us. Review our terms and privacy policy for account, payment and data information.
