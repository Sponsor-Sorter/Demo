Fairplay Control Room

A private operations dashboard for authorized Fairplay Gaming staff.

The Control Room brings game support, approved player entitlements and operational reports into one restricted workspace. It is separate from player sign-in and the Sponsor Sorter sponsorship platform.

Read-only AdMob reporting

The owner can connect Fairplay Gaming’s AdMob publisher account to review estimated ad earnings and performance for mapped games: Meadow Markets, Meadow Market Littles and Frontlines.

  • Filter reports by game and date, with results grouped by ad format and currency.
  • Review estimated earnings, impressions, ad requests, clicks and estimated eCPM.
  • Keep estimated earnings separate from confirmed payouts, game purchases and profit.

The Google integration requests the admob.report reporting permission. It does not use that permission to change ads, payment details or publisher settings. Google can delay report data by several hours.

Private access

Only the organization owner can configure or disconnect the Google connection. Reports are available to staff with the appropriate game and operations permissions. Staff use their existing private Control Room sign-in; this information page does not create an account or grant access.

Google authorization credentials are stored encrypted in the private backend. The reporting connection and data handling are described in the Control Room privacy notice.
